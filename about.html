About this site

This site offers a free Body Mass Index (BMI) calculator that works entirely in your browser. There is no signup, and the numbers you enter are never sent to our servers.

Why we built it

Many BMI calculators are cluttered with ads that cover the input fields, ask you to register, or are only available in English. We wanted a fast, clean, mobile-friendly tool that is available in more languages and respects your privacy.

How the calculation works

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres squared. We show your result against the categories published by the World Health Organization (WHO), your healthy weight range for your height, and short notes on the limits of BMI, such as muscle mass and differences between ethnic groups.

Important

BMI is a general screening tool, not a diagnosis. It does not measure body fat directly. For advice about your health, please speak to a qualified doctor or health professional.
